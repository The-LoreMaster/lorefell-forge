⚔ Weapons

Weapons are taken up at the Fell Forging or by investing an offensive attribute during a rest, up to three. Reforging happens during leveling, never here.

⛨ Armor

Armor is organized into three stances, each a complete defensive philosophy. Outside combat, stances change freely. In combat, changing stance is a React and takes your whole React, and all benefits swap at once.

Stances

Stance tiers unlock at armor levels 2, 6, and 10. Stance effects only run while the matching charge is lit: Tier 1 with one charge, Tier 2 with two, Tier 3 with three. When your charges clear, the effects fall silent. The Tier 1 bonus applies to your attributes automatically while active.

⛨ Lorebounds

Bonds form at the Fell Forging or by investing Vigor or Wit during a rest, up to two. Form follows level: Familiar, then Companion at 4, Corsair at 7.

❋ Skills

Tap a circle to set rank. Gold circles come from your lineage, origin, or motivation and cannot be cleared. At five filled circles a skill can be mastered: ranks return to 0, a mastery diamond is earned, and identity circles fall away. First mastery in a skill grants a talent.

⛨ Utilities

Tools, relics and consumables. Each is an Act, a React, a Passive or an Out of Combat use. A utility you have not yet identified shows only what it looks like in your hand until the LoreMaster reveals it. A few are known on sight and never hide.
