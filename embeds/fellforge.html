      What the Archive Does

      The Archive reads this Fell and writes a profile to play from. It also names the first impression they leave and gives tips for playing them.

      The Archive writes your Fell's Description. It goes onto your sheet in FellGlass as written. Consulting again replaces it.

      Continue without consulting and the Description stays empty.
